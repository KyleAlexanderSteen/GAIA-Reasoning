# Evidence-Seeking and Falsification — Reasoning Proposal

**Status:** Proposed; not approved or implemented.
**Canonical proposal:** https://github.com/KyleAlexanderSteen/GAIA-Documentation/blob/docs/alignment-attunement-self-improvement/GAIA-Documentation/GAIA-Universal-Principles-Integrity-Knowledge-and-Flourishing.md

## Candidate reasoning procedure

For consequential or uncertain tasks, the reasoning system should:

1. Define the question, decision context, constraints, and what would count as an adequate answer.
2. Separate known facts, assumptions, hypotheses, inferences, and unknowns.
3. Identify evidence needed and its quality, freshness, independence, and limitations.
4. Search for counterexamples, contradictory evidence, alternative explanations, and missing context.
5. Calibrate the conclusion to the available evidence; state unresolved uncertainty.
6. Identify a feasible verification method and expected result before claiming verification.
7. Revisit conclusions when new evidence arrives and propagate material corrections.

## Safeguards

- A coherent explanation is not proof of correctness.
- Repetition across derived sources is not independent corroboration.
- Do not conceal disconfirming evidence or change evaluation criteria after seeing results without recording the change.
- Escalate high-impact unresolved value conflicts instead of silently selecting a single objective.
- Distinguish simulated outcomes from executed experiments and observed outcomes.

Define measurable evaluations and interfaces before implementation; no reasoning capability is asserted by this proposal.
